# 一、新闻报道（News Report）

## 格式要求

标题（简洁明了）→ 导语（Who/What/When/Where/Why）→ 主体（事件过程、细节、各方反应）→ 结尾（事件影响、总结或展望）

## 核心句型

1. A grand ... Was held in ... On ..., attracting over ... Participants.
2. The activity, organized by ..., aimed to ....
3. According to an interview with ..., the event has not only ... But also ....
4. As the first of its kind in ..., it is expected to ....

## 模板框架

**Title:** ...

On ..., ... Took place at ... Under the sponsorship of .... The main goal of this activity was to ....

At the beginning of the event, .... Then, participants actively engaged in .... Many ... Commented that ....

This meaningful event came to an end at .... It not only ... But also laid a solid foundation for ..., leaving a deep impression on everyone present.

# 二、演讲稿（Speech）

## 格式要求

称呼（Dear teachers and classmates, / Ladies and gentlemen,）→ 开场问候 + 自我介绍（可选）+ 主题引入 → 主体（分论点 + 论据 / 例子）→ 结尾（总结 + 呼吁 / 祝福）→ 致谢（Thank you!）

## 核心句型

1. Good morning/afternoon, everyone! It's my great honor to stand here and share my views on ....
2. As we all know, ..., which is closely related to our daily life.
3. First and foremost, .... For instance, ....
4. What's more, .... We can learn from ... That ....
5. Let's take action to ..., and I believe ....

## 模板框架

Dear ...,

Good ...! Today, As a ..., I'd like to talk about ....

There's no doubt that ... Plays a vital role in .... Firstly, .... .... Secondly, .... ....

However, .... I appeal to us all to ...!

Finally, I wish all of you .... Thank you for listening!

# 三、感谢信（Thank-you Letter）

## 格式要求

称呼（Dear ...,）→ 开头（表明写信目的：致谢）→ 主体（具体感谢原因：事件经过、对方帮助的细节、带来的影响）→ 结尾（再次感谢 + 祝福）→ 落款（Yours sincerely, / Yours faithfully, + 署名 + 日期）

## 核心句型

1. I am writing to express my sincere gratitude to you for ....
2. Without your help/support, I could not have ....
3. I still remember clearly that ....
4. Your ... Has inspired me to ....
5. Please accept my deepest thanks again. Wish you all the best!

## 模板框架

Dear ...,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to convey my heartfelt thanks for your ... During ....

When I ..., you not only ... But also .... Thanks to your ..., I ... And .... Your ... Means a lot to me.

I would like to repay your kindness by .... Once again, please accept my sincere gratitude. May you ....

Yours sincerely,
...

# 四、投稿（Contribution）

## 格式要求

称呼（Dear Editors,）→ 开头（表明投稿意图 + 稿件主题）→ 主体（稿件核心内容：观点 / 故事 / 建议，分点或分层阐述）→ 结尾（表达期待发表的愿望 + 感谢审核）→ 落款（Yours truly, + 署名 + 联系方式 / 学校班级）

## 核心句型

1. I am writing to submit an article titled ... For your magazine/newspaper.
2. The article mainly focuses on ....
3. In the article, I share ....
4. I believe this topic is popular among your readers and can bring ....
5. I would be grateful if you could consider publishing my article. Looking forward to your reply!
6. I would appreciate it if you could take ... Into consideration.

## 模板框架

Dear Editors,

I am ... From .... I am writing to send you an article named ... In the hope that it can be published in your ....

.... In my article, I first ..., then .... ....

I think this article can ... And .... Thank you for taking the time to review my submission. I am looking forward to your positive response.

Yours truly,
...

# 五、求助信（Help-seeking Letter）

## 格式要求

称呼（Dear ...,）→ 开头（自我介绍 + 表明求助目的）→ 主体（说明遇到的问题：背景、具体困难、已尝试的解决方法及失败原因）→ 结尾（明确求助内容 + 表达感激 + 承诺反馈结果）→ 落款（Yours sincerely, + 署名 + 日期）

## 核心句型

1. I am ..., and I am writing to ask for your help with ....
2. Recently, I have been faced with a difficult situation: ....
3. I have tried ..., but unfortunately, they did not work.
4. I wonder if you could offer me ....
5. I would be extremely grateful for your kindness. I promise to inform you of the result once the problem is solved.

## 模板框架

Dear ...,

My name is ..., a ... From .... I am writing to seek your help because I am stuck in a problem that I cannot solve on my own.

.... Although I have ..., I still .... ....

I know you are good at .... Could you please ...? I would be deeply thankful for any of your help. I will try my best to follow your suggestions and will let you know the result in time.

Yours sincerely,
...

# 六、邀请信（Invitation Letter）

## 格式要求

称呼（Dear ...,）→ 开头（表明邀请意图 + 活动背景）→ 主体（活动细节：时间、地点、主题、流程；邀请原因：对方的重要性 / 相关性）→ 结尾（期待回复 + 感谢）→ 落款（Yours cordially, + 署名 + 日期）

## 核心句型

1. I am delighted to invite you to ....
2. The event will be held at ... On ... From ... To ..., with the theme of ....
3. We sincerely hope you can be present because ....
4. Please kindly let us know whether you can accept the invitation by ....
5. We are looking forward to your arrival with great excitement!

## 模板框架

Dear ...,

How are you doing? On behalf of ..., I am honored to invite you to our upcoming ....

The ... Is designed to .... It will take place at ... On ... From ... To .... The main arrangements include .... As ..., your presence will not only ... But also .... We would be grateful if you could ....

Could you please reply to this letter by ... To confirm your attendance? If you have any special requirements, please feel free to contact me at ....

We are eagerly looking forward to welcoming you!

Yours cordially,
...

# 七、建议信（Suggestion Letter）

## 格式要求

称呼（Dear ...,）→ 开头（表明写信目的：提出建议 + 说明建议背景）→ 主体（具体建议：分点列出，每条建议附理由 / 可行性）→ 结尾（表达希望建议被采纳的愿望 + 感谢考虑）→ 落款（Yours faithfully, + 署名 + 日期）

## 核心句型

1. I am writing to offer some constructive suggestions on ....
2. Having noticed that ..., I think some changes are needed.
3. Firstly, I suggest that ..., because ....
4. Secondly, it would be better if ..., as ....
5. I hope these suggestions will be taken into consideration. Thank you for your time and attention!

## 模板框架

Dear ...,

I am ... From .... I am writing to put forward some suggestions on ..., which I believe will bring ....

Recently, .... To solve these issues, I have the following suggestions: ...

I hope you will consider my suggestions carefully. .... Thank you for your time and efforts!

Yours faithfully,
...
